# Privacy Policy

*Effective August 18, 2026*

## The short version

FileHustle doesn't collect, store, or transmit any of your personal data — not to us, not to anyone. There are no accounts, no analytics, and no advertising. Everything happens directly between your device and the device you're sending to, over your own WiFi network.

## What stays on your device

Your device's broadcast name (a made-up name like "Tidy Yak") is generated fresh each launch and never stored. Any nicknames you give nearby devices, and your recent transfer history, are saved only on your device and never leave it.

## What happens during a transfer

Files move directly between your device and the device you choose, over your local WiFi network only. They never pass through any server operated by us or anyone else. Only devices on the same local network running FileHustle can see or receive anything from you — and only if you explicitly choose to send to them.

## What we don't do

No account or sign-up. No analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs. No data sold, shared, or transmitted to third parties. No data collected from children or anyone else — because none is collected at all.

## Changes to this policy

If anything about this changes, this policy will be updated and the date above will reflect that.
